NO LONGER DIVIDED: New Book Guides Readers from Internal War to Spiritual Alignment

[City, State] – [Date] – In a culture of constant performance, exhaustion, and quiet contradiction, author Elizabeth Rothschild releases No Longer Divided: From Internal War to Spiritual Alignment, a clear, honest guide for those who feel the friction of living two versions of themselves at once.

The book speaks directly to the invisible war most people carry alone: the voice of truth that knows what must change, and the voice of preservation that keeps returning to what is familiar—even when it is costly. Rothschild shows that the real problem is not lack of information. It is lack of integration. Without alignment, transformation cannot be sustained.

“You are not broken. But you are divided,” Rothschild writes. “And until that division is addressed, you will continue to feel the tension of trying to live in two directions at once.”

Drawing from lived experience and a deep understanding of how identity forms under pain, the book addresses:
• How identity fractures when pain is given authority
• The exhausting lie of earning worth through performance
• Why encounters with truth are invitations, not automatic change
• The practice of surrender as structural alignment, not loss of control
• How to interrupt the cycle of returning to familiar patterns
• The mechanics of division and the clarity that comes when contradiction is resolved

No Longer Divided is written for the person who is tired of pretending. It is not about becoming perfect overnight. It is about becoming whole—stopping the negotiation with what is already known to be untrue, and learning to live from truth with consistency.

The book is available now in Kindle and paperback through Amazon KDP.

About the Author
Elizabeth Rothschild (formerly known in ministry circles as Amanda Whatley) is a Christian counselor, humanitarian, and content creator. Her work includes anti-trafficking efforts, building safe houses, and years of advocacy for vulnerable girls and women. She brings both clinical training and hard-won spiritual clarity to the question of how people move from internal conflict to lasting alignment.
